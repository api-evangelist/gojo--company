# Gojo & Company, Inc.

> Tokyo-headquartered financial inclusion holding company that invests in, acquires and
> builds microfinance institutions and inclusive financial service providers across South
> Asia, Southeast Asia, Central Asia, the Caucasus and Africa. Founded 4 July 2014 by
> Taejun Shin and Sanjay Gandhi. Certified B Corporation. Private company; equity trades
> on secondary marketplaces.

## Important: there is no public API

Gojo & Company publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no API documentation, and no

- `https://gojo.co/openapi.json` -> 404

- `https://gojo.co/llms.txt` -> 404
- `api.gojo.co`, `developer.gojo.co`, `docs.gojo.co` -> do not resolve in DNS
- every `/.well-known/*` path on `gojo.co` and `www.pasio.io` -> 404

Gojo does build digital financial infrastructure — a cloud core banking platform and the Pasio
digital financial inclusion initiative — but that work is internal to the group and its partner
institutions, and no part of it is exposed as a public developer surface. Do not attempt to call
a Gojo API; none is published. Contact info@gojo.co for business enquiries.
